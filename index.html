Robinsons to Wed Abroad, Celebrate at Home

Two hearts, one name. The soon-to-be Mr. and Mrs. Robinson will exchange vows in Destination City and invite the people they love most to celebrate with them upon their return.

Above The couple, photographed on the shore where the story of this edition begins. Love, it turns out, photographs beautifully in black and white.

A Union Worth the Trip

In a ceremony set against the sea, two families will become one. For those who cannot make the journey abroad, the Robinsons have good news: the celebration is coming home. A reception in their honor will gather church family, friends, and loved ones for an evening of dinner, fellowship, and the kind of well-wishes that run a little long because there is simply too much to say.

This special edition serves as your official save the date. Fold it, frame it, or pin it to the refrigerator. Then turn to the final page and let the couple know you will be there.

What to Expect

An evening built around the two things every good celebration needs: good people and a grateful heart. Expect a warm welcome, a full table, joyful music, and a couple who will want to greet every single person in the room.

Attire is semi-formal. Think linen, florals, and sea breeze. Full details, including the venue and time, are printed in the reply section below.

Editorial

Looking in the Same Direction

From the Society Desk · By the Sea

Two are better than one, the Preacher wrote, because they have a good reward for their labour. There is a certain way two people stand when they have already decided on forever. Shoulders close. Eyes fixed on the same horizon. The photographs in this edition were not staged so much as caught.

What follows is a record of one afternoon on the shore, and the beginning of a much longer story.
